Conversar com a equipe ↗
What is AI specialized in sales for online stores?

It is AI that uses your store's catalog, information, and commercial rules to understand questions, recommend products, and guide the journey to checkout. Your company continues to define policies, prices, and commercial terms.

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI sales agent?

A chatbot can answer predefined questions. An AI sales agent uses catalog, customer, and operational context to find options, explain terms, and guide the next action within the store's rules.

What happens when I click subscribe?

Your selected plan takes you to the dashboard. Create your access or sign in, review the terms, and confirm the subscription in Stripe. Then you return to the dashboard with your session preserved. The paid plan activates after payment confirmation. If you already have a subscription, make changes in the billing portal.

What makes a store autonomous?

Zyon brings together catalog, AI-assisted service, recommendations, and the purchase journey. Customers can move forward without waiting for a manual response at every step. Your company continues to define products, policies, and commercial terms.

How does Zyon apply price, discount, and shipping rules?

Offers follow the rules configured by the store. Discount and margin limits are validated by commercial engines; price and shipping are considered before a condition is presented to the customer.

Do I need a technology team?

You manage catalog, identity, service, and rules through the dashboard. API resources and protocols are available for operations that need a more customized technical implementation.

How do I choose between Free, Growth, and Scale?

Free includes up to 100 confirmed purchases per month. Growth includes up to 500, voice, a knowledge base, advanced rules, and a custom domain. Scale includes purchases without a monthly limit, experimentation, and Revenue Manager. For high AI usage and special integrations, check the terms.

What is charged besides the monthly fee?

The store's Zyon fee is R$ 2.99 per transaction on Free, R$ 1.49 on Growth, and R$ 0.99 on Scale. The buyer pays R$ 0.99 in service per purchase. Payment-provider fees are separate. On Free, the first 14 days have no store Zyon transaction fee.

Does my store keep my brand?

Yes. The service name, theme, and identity can be customized. Growth and Scale allow the Zyon badge to be removed. Growth and Scale include a custom domain.
