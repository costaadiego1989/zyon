## Implementation boundaries

- Keep the existing canonical WhatsApp intake, verified turn, binding epoch, claim and request-start fences. Existing LLM `escalate_to_human` uses server-owned scope; the model never supplies tenant, recipient, session or a delivery privilege. T57 tests natural paraphrases, authenticated voice, explicit negation/quotation and ambiguous professional-versus-attendant requests. A successful model-triggered handoff must produce the canonical acknowledgement after intake; do not label an arbitrary AI response as an authorized handoff notice.
- Store the fallback recipient in a small private per-merchant support-notification configuration, separate from `SupportSetting` and public `storeSettings`. Reuse the authorized support settings surface. Saving/clearing a phone preserves FAQ row/content/updatedAt, source versions, work and chunks; saving FAQ preserves the phone. Do not borrow `budgetWhatsapp`, company phone or the connected sender automatically.
- Create one durable alert purpose per confirmed new ticket/epoch. Receipt creation shares the canonical case transaction; rollback creates neither alert nor false acknowledgement. Dispatch happens afterwards. Reuse outbox, authenticated merchant channel lookup, normalization and provider eligibility conventions, retaining a separate receipt from operator messages, buyer handoff notices and order notices. No fabricated orderId or paid-order notification type.
- Send a short staff notice containing the case reference and a dashboard link requiring sign-in/tenant permission. Exclude conversation text, clinical content and private buyer data. Missing/disconnected channel or unmet session/template eligibility becomes an honest unavailable state. Provider acceptance differs from delivery/read; uncertain external attempts are not resent automatically. Local fixtures capture transport and never contact Meta/Twilio/Bubble.
- Query `ServiceAppointment` for Agendamentos. Keep `pending`, `scheduled`, `in_service`, `completed`, `no_show`, `cancelled` distinct from all financial states. An order without a canonical appointment is not a card. Multiple appointments linked to one order remain separate. List and Kanban use the same result/filters and truthful total/pagination, including pending/cancelled history.
- Reuse Orders visual controls and dashboard tokens, not its fulfillment data model. Show scheduled local date/time, professional, service, customer and a separate financial badge. Versioned permitted actions and keyboard/button alternatives call the same canonical commands as the management drawer. A conflict retains the original card and finance; no drag infers attendance, charges/refunds or deletes history.
- Keep Atendimento as service/professional/availability/policy management. Services receive Agendamentos as the daily primary entry; products retain Orders. Mode, role and plan remain independent. Guard direct URLs, reload/back, mobile navigation, command palette and merchant switches. Preserve stored historical orders and an authorized history access path. Explicit historical backfill belongs to T84.
